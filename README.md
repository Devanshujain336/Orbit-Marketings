# Orbit Marketing Engine

Orbit is an end-to-end marketing engine for startups/businesses that handles everything from content creation to lead qualification, so founders don't have to juggle five agencies.

How it works:

Onboarding & Analysis — Business adds their website + startup info. Orbit analyzes the brand's visual identity and vibe.

Content Creation — Business chooses:

Offline shoot → Orbit collaborates with VasuDev MarketX for filming + editing

AI-generated → Orbit creates videos by identifying viral content patterns and adapting them to match the startup's specific vibe

Distribution — Videos get scheduled and ad-boosted on Instagram/Facebook to reach a larger audience, driving inbound leads via WhatsApp/Instagram DMs.

Lead Qualification — Automated WhatsApp/DM replies engage incoming leads and sort them into high/medium/low value tiers, so the business only spends personal time on high-value leads instead of manually sifting through hundreds of DMs.

The core value prop: instant response to every customer (boosting conversion) + founders only spend time on leads worth their time (saving hours of manual triage).
